9 grandchildren, 7 great-grandchildren total as of 2005. Names not listed individually in obituary.

⭐ Carey Family Line — Indiana → Iowa → Kansas → California
Joel Thornburg Carey (b. 7 May 1832 Carmel, Hamilton Co. Indiana, d. 2 Apr 1915 Indiana) married Elizabeth Stanley (b. ~1831/1832 Indiana, d. 11 Jan 1927) in 1854 Indiana. Had 6 recorded children: Serepta, Niley H., Lyman W., Luther G., Mima, George Stanley.
Luther G. Carey (b. 1867 Indiana, d. 1897 Altadena CA) married Millie Ruth (b. 16 Nov 1868 Illinois, d. 12 Apr 1937 LA Co). Their son Francis Gray Carey (b. 4 Feb 1897 Pasadena) connects directly to the Hewitt/Greeran line.
Francis Gray Carey married Daisy Beatrice Carey (b. 19 Sep 1904 Texas, d. 20 Jan 1989 Monrovia, CA). Their daughter Louise Frances Carey married Maurice Frederick Hewitt, whose daughter Jean Belle married Gerard Greeran.
George Stanley Carey (b. 30 Nov 1874 Iowa, d. 30 Apr 1936 Manhattan, KS) married Annett Carey (b. 26 Aug 1876 Columbus City, Iowa, d. 24 Dec 1908 Pomona, CA). Their daughter Georgia Elizabeth became Georgia Emmons, linking to the Emmons-Pearson Kansas line.
Niley H. Carey (b. ~1864 Indiana) married Irene Y. Yates (b. 16 Dec 1865 Iowa, d. 16 Dec 1961 Sonoma Co. CA). In 1950 both were living in Sonoma, CA.

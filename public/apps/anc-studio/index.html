Research Notes

Applied best practices from current Web Audio documentation.

2026 build
1. AudioWorklet for real-time DSP

Adaptive mode runs off the main thread for lower glitch risk than deprecated ScriptProcessor patterns.

2. Biquad frequency targeting

Low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, and notch filters let you isolate steady crowd rumble, AC hum, or hiss before inversion.

3. Delay compensation

Because ANC depends on timing alignment, the app includes a small output delay adjustment window to experiment with phase arrival.

4. Safety limiter

A compressor/limiter after the anti-noise path helps tame sudden peaks and reduce clipping risk.

Safety + Reality Check

This demo cannot replace certified hearing protection. Web ANC is limited by microphone placement, headphone leakage, device latency, and the lack of a true error microphone at the ear.

  • Best for steady low-frequency noise like HVAC, transit rumble, or crowd hum.
  • Not fast enough to reliably cancel fireworks, gunshots, or other sharp transients.
  • Use sealed wired headphones and keep output levels conservative.
